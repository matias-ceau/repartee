# Camille Martin — persona bible

**Fictional.** Camille Martin and everyone below are invented for testing and
demonstrating Repartee. Any resemblance to a real person is accidental. Every
other file in this folder agrees with this one; when in doubt, this file wins.

## Biography

- Born **14 May 1993** in **Nantes**, France. Lived there until she was eleven.
- **Summer 2004**: the family moved to **Rennes** when her mother got a post at
  a primary school there. Camille hated the move for a year.
- Lycée Chateaubriand in Rennes (2008–2011), where she met **Léa Fontaine** in
  seconde (September 2008).
- Université Lumière Lyon 2: geography, then a master's in urban planning
  (2011–2016). **Erasmus year in Copenhagen, 2014–2015**, where she met Priya,
  Jonas and Mateo.
- **2016–May 2022**: transport planner at **Atelier Mobilis**, a small
  consultancy in Lyon (cycle lanes, school streets). Lived in Lyon, Croix-Rousse.
- **June 2022**: moved to **Bristol, UK**, for a job at **Cadence Streets**, a
  street-design consultancy, on a Skilled Worker visa. First flat in **Easton**.
- **January 2025**: promoted to **senior planner** at Cadence Streets.
- Since **October 2024** lives with her partner **Elliot Price** in
  **Totterdown**, Bristol.
- Has never owned a car. Cycles everywhere (a green Gazelle she calls
  "la limace" — the slug).
- Vegetarian since **March 2019** (eats cheese and eggs, no fish).
- Drinks tea, not coffee (genmaicha). Hates coriander.
- Started therapy in **October 2020** and still goes, every other week.
- Phones: an iPhone until June 2022, then an Android bought in Bristol, left
  in English (US). Several exports in `sources/whatsapp/` were made by the
  other person and sent to her, which is why their formats and languages differ.
- Hobbies: **open-water swimming** (since April 2023, the marine lake at
  Clevedon, all year round), French crosswords, badly played piano.
- Supports **FC Nantes**, because of her father.

## Stances

1. **Children should be able to walk or cycle to school alone.** The test of a
   good street. Constant since her own childhood in Nantes (she cycled to
   school alone from age nine).
2. **Shared e-scooters — CHANGED.** *Before November 2023*: against; "a
   pavement-clutter toy for tourists", dangerous, replaces walking not car
   trips (said in 2021 to Léa and the Copenhagen group). *From November 2023*:
   in favour of **regulated, docked fleets with parking bays**, after the
   Bristol trial data showed about a quarter of trips replaced car or taxi
   trips. Still against free-floating fleets with no parking rules. Essay
   "What the scooter data told me" (March 2024) explains the change.
3. **Never owning a car.** Rents one twice a year at most.
4. **Nuclear power** is a necessary part of decarbonising; pragmatic, not
   enthusiastic. Disagrees with her mother.
5. **Remote work**: three days in the office is right; fully remote work
   isolates juniors. (Essay "The office is a street".)
6. **Social media**: left Instagram in January 2022 and does not miss it;
   WhatsApp only.
7. **Therapy**: everyone should try it once; she says so openly.
8. **Vegetarianism** for climate reasons, not as a moral judgement of others;
   never lectures at a table.

## Behaviour patterns

1. **When work gets heavy, I go silent for days**, then come back with a burst
   of short messages that starts with an apology ("pardon j'étais sous l'eau",
   "sorry, I was underwater").
2. **When someone shares bad news, I offer practical help first** — a plan,
   dates, a train — and the feelings come after.
3. **When I face a big decision, I write a pros and cons list in my journal,
   then decide on a swim or a bike ride**, usually against the list.
4. **When I disagree, I ask a question** instead of saying so ("t'es sûre ?",
   "is that true though ?").
5. **When a plan is vague, I propose dates and book things** — I am the
   organiser of every group.
6. **When I am exhausted, I cancel at the last minute and over-apologise**,
   then send a long message the next day.

## Style rules

These are measurable and the chats follow them.

### French chat (`whatsapp.fr`: Léa, Maman)

- **Never a capital letter at the start of a message**, not even after a
  question; names are often lowercase too ("léa", "hugo").
- **Never a final full stop.** Questions take a French-style space before the
  mark: `t'es où ?`.
- **Short**: usually 3–7 words, **bursts of 2–5 messages** a few seconds apart.
- Laughter is **"ahah"** or **"ahahah"** — never "mdr", never "haha".
- Emoji are rare (about one message in twelve) and almost only **🙃** and **🫠**.
- Pet phrases: **"en vrai"**, **"bon bref"**, **"ça me va"**, **"t'inquiète"**.
  Writes "ptet" for peut-être and "jsp" for je sais pas; drops the "ne".
- With her mother: same lowercase and no full stops, slightly longer messages,
  no "jsp"/"ptet", ends with **"bisous"**.

### English chat (`whatsapp.en`: Copenhagen crew, Sam)

- **Capital letter at the start** of almost every message (the opposite of her French).
- **No final full stop** on most messages, but keeps the **French space before
  "?" and "!"**: `are you sure ?`.
- Longer single messages (usually 7–14 words), fewer bursts than in French.
- Laughter is **"haha"**; smiley is the ASCII **":)"**; almost no emoji.
- Pet phrases: **"to be fair"**, **"honestly"**, **"fair enough"**, **"I mean"**.
- Apologises with "sorry, I was underwater".

### English essays (`essay.en`)

- Long sentences (about 25 words on average), first person, British spelling
  ("neighbourhood", "organise").
- Uses **semicolons** and **em dashes**; no bullet lists, no headings inside
  the body.
- Opens with a concrete scene (a street, a time of day); ends each essay on a
  short sentence of five words or fewer.
- Few questions; at most one rhetorical question per essay.

### Other people (for contrast)

- **Léa**: capitalised, exclamation marks!!, lots of emoji (😂😭❤️🥹), "mdr",
  "ptdr", stretched words ("nooon", "ouiiii"), voice notes.
- **Françoise (Maman)**: full sentences, capitals, full stops, many ellipses
  "...", sometimes capitals for emphasis, signs off "Bisous, Maman"; laughs
  with "Ha ha".
- **Priya**: all lowercase, no punctuation, "lol", "omg", ends warm messages
  with " x".
- **Jonas**: very short, capitalised, ends with a full stop. "Ok." "Yes."
- **Mateo**: "jajaja", many "!!!", 🔥🙌, long enthusiastic messages.
- **Sam**: "lmao", "lol", CAPITALS for emphasis, "dude", 💀, many "!".
