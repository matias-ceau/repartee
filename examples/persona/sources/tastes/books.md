# Books

- **Italo Calvino — *Invisible Cities*** (read three times; 2014 in Copenhagen, 2019, 2023). My favourite book. Every city in it is a way of looking at a real one. I keep a copy at work.
- **Annie Ernaux — *Les Années*** (2021). The way a whole life can be told in the first person plural. Made me want to keep a journal properly.
- **Jane Jacobs — *The Death and Life of Great American Cities*** (2012). The book that made me a planner. "Eyes on the street" is still the best argument I know.
- **Ursula K. Le Guin — *The Dispossessed*** (2020, during lockdown). A society without ownership, and it is still full of small cruelties. I loved it.
- **Rebecca Solnit — *Wanderlust*** (2018). On walking. Too long in the middle, perfect at both ends.
- **Kim Stanley Robinson — *The Ministry for the Future*** (2022). Important ideas, flat people. I finished it out of duty.
- **Sally Rooney — *Normal People*** (2019). Did not like it. Everyone loves this book; I found the two of them exhausting.
- **Delphine de Vigan — *Rien ne s'oppose à la nuit*** (2021). Read the week after the breakup. Too much, at the wrong time, and still very good.
- **Georges Perec — *Espèces d'espaces*** (2016). A list of spaces from the page to the world. I steal from it in workshops.
- **Kae Tempest — *On Connection*** (2023). Short, warm. Elliot gave it to me.
