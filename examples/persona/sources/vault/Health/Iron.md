# Iron deficiency

## Results
- 28 Nov 2022, GP blood test (Bristol), after months of tiredness and a 3pm slump: ferritin **9 µg/L** (range 15–150), haemoglobin 118 g/L, slightly low.
- 6 Mar 2023, repeat: ferritin **41 µg/L**, haemoglobin 131 g/L. Back to normal.

## Treatment
- Ferrous sulphate 200 mg once a day for three months (Dec 2022 – Feb 2023).
- Take it on an empty stomach with orange juice; **not with tea** (Léa's advice, and the GP's): tea blocks absorption.
- Side effects: stomach ache the first two weeks, then fine.

## What I changed
- Lentils, chickpeas, tofu most days; vitamin C with meals.
- Genmaicha moved to between meals.
- Vegetarian since 2019, so the GP said this is common and not a reason to stop.
