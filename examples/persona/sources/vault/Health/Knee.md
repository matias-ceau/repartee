# Right knee — MCL sprain

## Injury
- 8 Feb 2024, around 6pm, Cheltenham Road: a parked car's door opened in front of me ("dooring"). Went over the door, landed on the right knee.
- A&E at the BRI: medial collateral ligament sprain, **grade 2**. No fracture on X-ray. No surgery.

## Recovery
- Hinged brace and crutches for three weeks (off crutches 1 Mar 2024).
- Physiotherapy from 14 Feb to **17 May 2024**, weekly, then every two weeks. Physio very strict about the exercises.
- Back on the bike 18 May 2024, by another route.

## Notes
- Swelling and bruising worst on days 3–5.
- Still a slight ache going downhill on cold mornings.
- Reported the incident to the council's road safety team; the door zone on Cheltenham Road is now in our slides at [[Cadence Streets]].
