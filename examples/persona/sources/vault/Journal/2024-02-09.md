# 9 February 2024

Je l'écris en anglais parce que tout s'est passé en anglais : A&E, the nurse, the forms.

Yesterday, Cheltenham Road, about 6pm. A parked car, a door opening, me going over it. I remember the sound more than the fall. Right knee, MCL sprain, grade 2. Crutches. See [[Knee]].

Elliot came to get me and was calm the whole evening, which made me calm. He made tea every forty minutes as if it were medicine. Maybe it is.

J'ai passé dix ans à dessiner des pistes hors de la "door zone". Je sais exactement pourquoi c'est arrivé. Ça ne m'aide pas du tout.
