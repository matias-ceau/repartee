# 5 October 2024

Moved in with [[Elliot Price|Elliot]] today. A narrow house in Totterdown, painted the colour of a mint sweet, with a workshop at the end of the garden for his kilns.

His potter friends carried everything. I carried the plants and the organisation: a spreadsheet of boxes, numbered, with a room for each. Elliot laughed at it and then used it all day.

Ce soir il a fait son dal et on a mangé par terre. J'ai eu peur pendant des semaines et ce soir je n'ai pas peur du tout.
