# Bristol e-scooter trial — evaluation

- Contract won January 2023; final report to the council in November 2023.
- Data: about 140,000 trips plus a rider survey. Analysis with Sam, two weeks in October–November 2023.
- Key finding: **26% of trips** (distance-weighted) replaced a car or taxi trip. Strongest in the evenings and on hilly routes badly served by buses.
- Recommendation: keep shared scooters with **regulated, docked fleets**, speed caps and **mandatory parking bays**. No free-floating fleets.
- This changed my own view; before, I was against shared scooters. See [[2023-11-20]] and [[What the scooter data told me]].
